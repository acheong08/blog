This site was initially created as a semi-anonymous blog, where I, without a
real public identity could post whatever I wanted without consequence. Well, my
life has taken a bit of an unexpected turn, and privacy is no longer possible.

Once on the internet, content can almost never be hidden away again. I won't
delete my old posts, but they'll be archived under [/archive](/archive) with the
disclaimer that those old musings no longer represent my current views & does
not intend to defame. I've deleted _some_ posts but honestly there shouldn't be
anything too outrageous that anyone would wanna look into. For personal posts,
I'll be migrating to post on a new anonymous domain. Obviously not gonna link
it.

## A Brief History

The original `duti.site` was started sometime in 2016 by my then senior,
Benjamin Hollon at Dalat International School (Penang, Malaysia). It had a few
things, namely an HTML tutorial, a few games, and wizard.css — something akin to
Bootstrap but with a minimalist aesthetic.

While I had learned a bit of Scratch and _some weird magnetic tape robotic
thing_ before, this was where I learned web programming, and also the beginning
of the time I had unfettered internet access.

Of course, being the Chūnibyō kids we were (2nd year of middle school), there
was also a `projectwd.duti.site`, which stood for "Project World Domination" 🤦.
Memories are a bit fuzzy about exactly what we did, but I remember learning
about the dark web via Tor, sending spoofed emails (SPF/DKIM was rarely seen
back then), and various tidbits of Batch to remotely shut down library
computers. I created my first GitHub account around that time, but the username
has been lost to time.

Sometime in 2018, the domain expires, and given that GitHub's Education pack
allowed you to get a free `.me` or `.site` domain, I snatched it up, but didn't
actually do anything with it... It ended up in the hands of squatters shortly
after.

At some point, a family member wanted to start an internet business with a
decently short and memorable domain name. I bought the domain `duti.dev` since
`.site` wasn't available and `dev` was well priced. It was gonna be a site to
sell organic vegetables from my grandparent's village, but considering I
wouldn't be available as tech support for most of the year, they ended up just
using Google Forms instead.

Starting in 2023, I wrote a few blogs on random things I encountered with Linux.
Somehow I accidentally deleted the GitHub repository for the blog in 2025 with
no local backups. Most of the content was unrecoverable, though some copies live
on in Cardiff University's Discord in the form of a PDF (e.g. a tutorial on how
to get non-Ubuntu Linuxes connected to eduroam). Migrated to `gozer` and started
doing proper backups from then on :)

I got into a bit of trouble with earlier drafts on the post criticizing
HackEurope. Lots of professional connections reached out and warned me of
burning bridges & inappropriate language. I stopped posting as much since, as I
hate having to consider every word and how I may be perceived as a result. I
missed being a random anonymous person from a 3rd world country where internet
laws don't apply and nobody could find me anyways.

## So What Now?

I suppose I'll start treating this site like I do LinkedIn. A somewhat official
place to put corporatized posts.
