Within a blink of an eye, culture has evolved to an unrecognizable point. Mass
psychosis over "brain rot". Inexplicable, random hand motions associated with
numbers, some AI-generated monstrosity with a baseball bat, and questionable
children's animations. Even in the most stale professional environment, I've
seen someone pitch that their knowledge of "Rizz" makes them a better founder.

It's not even an issue of "kids these days". It is coming from my generation,
and even people much older. My parents, and even grandparents have now become
addicted to short form AI content. These were conspiracy theorists who used to
decry the implementation of 5G out of cancer concerns and despised technology.

I do not remember these _things_ existing before the COVID era.

Have I become an alien or have aliens replaced everyone I ever knew?
